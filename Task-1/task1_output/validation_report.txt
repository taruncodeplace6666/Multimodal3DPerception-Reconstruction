Projection pipeline
-------------------
  1. Load Velodyne scan  (N×4, XYZR)
  2. Filter X_lidar > 0  (forward hemisphere)
  3. Ground & ego-vehicle filters
  4. Homogeneous coords  →  (N,4)
  5. P_cam = T_cam2_velo · P_velo
  6. Filter Z_cam > 0  (points in front of camera)
  7. p = K_cam2 · P_cam[:3]
  8. (u,v) = (p[0]/p[2], p[1]/p[2])
  9. Filter to image bounds
 10. Colour by depth (red=close → green → blue=far)
